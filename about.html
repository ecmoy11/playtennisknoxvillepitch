A city program,
not a private club

Play Tennis Knoxville is run by City of Knoxville Parks & Recreation. There is no membership, no initiation fee and no waiting list. You can walk onto a court for $5 or sign up for a class that starts from nothing at all.

By the numbers

53 public courts across the city
2 staffed centers open seven days
Ages 2 to adult, every level

The person who runs it

Deidra Dunn

Deidra is the City Tennis Director. She and her staff teach a full slate of classes and clinics every week, from two-year-olds swinging at a foam ball with a parent on court, to adults who have never held a racquet and are quietly nervous about it.

The through line is the same at every age. Tennis is a game you can play your whole life, so the job is to get people started and keep them going. That is why the first step is cheap and low-stakes, why the groups are built around what you can actually do rather than how old you are, and why there is always a next rung once you are ready for it.
