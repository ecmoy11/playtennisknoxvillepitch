Junior Lessons & Clinics

Six-week group clinics for ages two to eighteen, grouped by age and ball colour. Saturdays at Tyson Park, Thursdays at West Hills.

How to sign up

  1. Pick the class that matches your child’s age from the table above.
  2. Fill in the enrolment form, one form per child, per class.
  3. You will get a confirmation email with the court number and what to bring.

Good to know

Little Hot Shots needs one parent or grandparent on court for the whole class.

Pre-enrolment is required. Classes fill by age group, and walk-ups cannot be added on the day.
