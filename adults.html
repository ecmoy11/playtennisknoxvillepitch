Adults

Never held a racquet, or back after twenty years, or looking for a league. One ladder takes you from a first lesson to match play, and you can step on at any rung.

Start where you are

Four levels. Each one assumes you finished the one before it, but nobody checks, so pick the description that sounds like you.

Level 1Tennis 101Never played. Six hours of fast instruction and you finish able to play a match. Level 2Tennis 201Finished 101. Same ground again with much more live ball play. Level 3Tennis 301You can serve, rally and keep score. Sharpening all three. Level 4Tennis 301+Finished 301. Each session takes one theme and works it. Approval needed.

How to sign up

  1. Pick the level that matches the description, not the one that sounds impressive.
  2. Fill in the enrolment form. One form per person, per class.
  3. You will get a confirmation email with the court number and what to bring.

Good to know

A racquet comes with Tennis 101. You do not need to buy anything to start.

301+ needs the tennis director’s approval. Email first and say which session of 301 you finished.

Pre-enrolment is required for every clinic. Walk-ups cannot be added on the day.
