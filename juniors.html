Juniors

Ages two to eighteen, from a first foam ball with a parent on court to school league match play. Classes run at Tyson Park and West Hills Park.

Start with their age

Groups follow the standard ball progression, slower and lower-bouncing balls on smaller courts first.

Ages 2–3Little Hot ShotsFoam ball, parent on court the whole class. Ages 4–5Junior TotsMovement, hand-eye and the basics, red and foam balls. Ages 5–8Red AcesScaled courts and low-compression balls.
